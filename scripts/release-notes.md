## Download

Download **PEPE-PTZ-Controller.zip** below, unzip it, and drag **PEPE PTZ Controller** into your Applications folder.
Works on macOS 13 Ventura or newer, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

## First time you open it

The app isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the first time:

1. Double-click the app. macOS says it can't verify it. Click **Done**.
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to PEPE PTZ Controller.
3. Confirm with your password. After that it opens normally.

When asked, allow it to find devices on your **local network**, or it can't reach the cameras.
